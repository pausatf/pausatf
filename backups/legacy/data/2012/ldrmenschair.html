Pacific Association USATF Seeks Men's LDR Chair


Dear PA/USATF Long Distance Running Members and Clubs:


You are receiving this e-mail because PA/USATF is seeking an individual interested in assuming the role of Men's Long Distance Running Committee Chair (a volunteer position). Your current Men's LDR Committee Chair is Tyler Abbott who, after many years of dedicated service, wishes to step down from this position (and also as "primary" LDR Chair who oversees all operations of the LDR Committee). Individuals interested in the Men's LDR Committee Chair position will, initially, serve as an apprentice under the tutelage of Mr. Abbott until an agreed-upon apprentice period is completed. At that time, the individual will assume (by formal committee election) the role of Men's LDR Committee Chair.

Three committee chairs lead PA/USATF LDR activities: Open Men's LDR Committee Chair, Open Women's LDR Committee Chair, and Masters LDR Committee Chair. These chairs serve for two-year terms with no term limits. Typically, one of these chairs is selected to oversee all LDR activities as a whole and is appointed by combined LDR committee proclamation as the main "LDR Chair."

The primary responsibilities of the Open Men's LDR Committee Chair are:

  • Provide competitive opportunities for open men's long distance runners by working with the main LDR Chair, Women's and Masters LDR Committee Chairs, Cross Country and MUT/Ultrarunning Subcommittee Chairs, LDR Executive Committee, LDR Grand Prix Coordinators, PA/USATF LDR member clubs, event directors, and other PA/USATF members as necessary.
  • Serve as a sounding board for the needs of the open men's LDR community.
  • Attend as many as possible combined PA/USATF Open Men's/Open Women's/Masters LDR Committee meetings, held approximately four times annually at selected PA/USATF LDR Championship/Grand Prix events.
  • Attend as many as possible PA/USATF Board of Athletics meetings held five times a year in either Foster City, Walnut Creek, or Sacramento.
  • Make a visible presence at as many PA/USATF LDR Championships/Grand Prix events as possible, and serve PA/USATF athletes and the hosting event organization as necessary at these events.
  • Work with the combined LDR Committee and Main LDR Chair to elect/designate a PA/USATF representative who sits on the USATF national Mens LDR Committee and attends the USATF Annual Meeting (as a PA/USATF delegate) held at the end of November each year in a location rotated around the country. A travel stipend is provided to this representative/delegate to help defray costs.
  • Participate in the creation of an annual PA/USATF LDR budget along with the other LDR Chairs and combined LDR Committee.

For additional information concerning the Men's LDR Committee, click here to open the Pacific Association Bylaws (http://www.pausatf.org/data/2010/pabylaws5.18.10.pdf) and refer to article 10 on Sport Committees.

If you are interested in the position of PA/USATF Men's Long Distance Running Chair, know of someone who might be interested, or would like further information, please email current chair Tyler Abbott and cc LDR Representative Mark Winitz.

Please include the candidate's name, e-mail, and phone number. It would also be helpful to include a brief 100-150 word statement about yourself and your current participation in PA/USATF long distance running programs.

Thank you for considering a key contribution to our Association and our athletes in this important leadership position!
