Press Release - Winter All-Comers Series Slated @ Diablo Valley College (DVC)

January 3, 10, 17, 24, 31

DIABLO VALLEY COLLEGE WILL HOST Winter ALL-COMER Track & Field SERIES, meets will feature steeplechase (2000 and 3000 meter events, depending upon interest). DVC features a new 400 Meter Rekortan track surface and jumping area.

Winter All-comer track meet series is slated to begin with five (5) meets in January, beginning January 3rd.  All meets to begin at 2:00pm, Field Events, 2:30pm. DVC Head Coach Daniel Cruz has announced that DVC will host an all-comer track & field series with the steeplechase listed as a feature event each week. The Men and Women's Hammer Throw will not be contested in the winter series.

Go to www.directathletics.com to register. Follow instructions for registering as an individual then go to the posted meet you wish to enter and follow the entry instructions. Pay your entry fees when you arrive at the meet. You must be registered on Directathletics!
