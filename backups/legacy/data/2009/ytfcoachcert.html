Dear all,
 
MacCanDo Tenderloin Youth Track Club is pleased to announce that two of our coaches (Robert McDaniels and Yuko Hayashi) have been certified by USATF as Level 1 Track & Field coach.
 
Two coaches were granted the scholorships by USA Track & Field Pacific Association, finished the certification course and passed the test.
