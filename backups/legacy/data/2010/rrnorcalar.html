Pending AR for Linda Somers Smith

 

From Tom Bernhard

Linda Somers Smith  set a pending American Reocrd for Women 45-49 at the NorCal 10-Miler, in Redding CA on Saturday, March 6, 2010

According to the USATF Records  site:
http://www.usatf.org/statistics/records/view.asp?division=american&location=road&age=masters&ageGroup=45-49&sport=LDR
The current AR for W45-49 is held by Barbara Filutze at  59:15.

Today Linda ran 57:09, over two minutes under  the old record.
