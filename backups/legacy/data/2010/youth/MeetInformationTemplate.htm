2010 Junior Olympic Track & Field Forms - Meet Information Template

Meet Information Template

  • The meet information template is intended to serve as a guide for supplying specific meet information (date, location, directions, schedule, etc) to athletes, parents, and coaches. 

  • The template has been set up so that you can replace all items noted in red font and serves as a concise way to provide a substantial amount of information to participants.  After you update the items in red font be sure to change the font color to black.  The red font is only there to provide a key as to what content may need to be changed to reflect the specifics of your event.

  • Once updated with your specific meet information, it can be posted to your website alongside the Individual and Team Entry Forms.
