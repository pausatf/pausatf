PA XC No Service Fee Online Registrations Through USATF Open Now until Friday November 19 at Noon!



It might rain, the lines will be long for race-day reg-- stay dry and sign up online for PA XC. 

Note-- there is NO SERVICE FEE (so it's cheaper than the registration you can also do on Active.com), and unlike Active, which closes Wednesday midnight, registration is open later-- to Friday noon.  Early reg fee of $25 holds until that time, after that it's race day reg at $30, cash or check.  

You will need a USATF membership number (if you have forgotten it, there is an easy search at the site, also you can make sure you are currently a member, and eligible to score for yourself and your team).  

Results will be computerized this year, so signing up online ahead of time also helps with quick printing of results (unofficial as always until protest period is over).  We can use a little help here...  quick posting of results is also part of the generous deal provided by USATF's Travel Grant program to Nationals-- see separate item below.

Here's where you go. Registration is the same process as other USATF championships at national website.

Individual Entry Form <http://www.usatf.org/events/entry/?eventID=C10060572>
Team Entry Form <http://www.usatf.org/events/entry/?eventID=C10060572&amp;mode=team>  (batch entries by club administrator)

And while you are registering for PAs, why not sign up for nationals?

$500 Travel Fund Awarded Winning Men's and Women's Teams at PA Cross to go to Nationals

As part of USATF’s effort to support Association-based programs as well as the development of the Open Club system, a travel grant program will be offered in 2010.   The top men's and women's team at PA XC will each receive a $500 stipend for travel expenses to the 2010 USATF National Club Cross Country Championships in Charlotte, N.C.  Link in case you didn't see it 

Spread the word-- race for PA cash, medals, honor, and also travel funds to get you to Nationals.  Race hard! 

Another reason to sign up for PA XC now!
