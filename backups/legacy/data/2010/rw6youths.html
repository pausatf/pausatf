Six PA Youth Athletes Make the 2009 National Racewalk Honor Roll

The following youth athletes are being recognized by USATF - through the national racewalk committee - as the best racewalkers in each age group for the 2009 Calendar year. The ranking are based upon various performances during the year, including the USATF Junior Olympic and USATF Youth Nationals, and the results from events composing the USATF Race Walk Grand Prix for Junior Men and Women, such as the USATF Junior Nationals and the Americas Cup Trials.  These results shows the Pacific Association's strong commitment to racewalking and the continued training of our track athletes.
 
INTERMEDIATE BOYS
Tyler Sorensen - Pleasanton Heat Lafayette, CA

YOUTH BOYS
Daimon Todd - Umoja TC San Jose, CA

YOUTH GIRLS
Nicolette Sorensen - Pleasanton Heat Lafayette, CA
Lila Haba - LSI Sprint Saratoga, CA

MIDGET GIRLS
Caitlin Palacio - LSI Sprint
San Jose, CA

BANTAM BOYS
Jason Gomez - LSI Sprint San Jose, CA
