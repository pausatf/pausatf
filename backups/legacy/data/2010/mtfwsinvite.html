3rd Annual
Western States Masters International Invitational
UC Davis

July 9-11, 2010

from Mary Woo


Ed Burke Sets World and American Records!

Ed Burke (M70) set a World record in the hammer with a throw of 58.6 meters, and American records in both the weight (20.82m) and super weight (11.66m) throws! Ed's weight throw mark was just shy of the listed World record of 20.85!!!   

We also had many Nation-leading marks, including:

  • Karen Maxwell (W51),  80 and 300m hurdles
  • Carmel Papworth-Barnum (W44), 3000m run
  • Jeffrey Adkins (M49), 3000m run
  • Larry Barnum (M66), 400m dash
  • Bill Ballantine (M85), 400m dash
  • Wendy Pratt (W49), mile run
  • Donna Chan (W59), mile run
  • Al Shook (M70), mile run
  • Irene Obera (W76), 200m dash
  • Gary Sims (M72), 200m dash
  • Debbie Zakerski (W48), 2000m steeplechase
  • Cherrie Sherrard (W70), shot put
  • Peter Labarge (M71), discus throw, javelin throw
  • Jan Anderson (W63), javelin throw
