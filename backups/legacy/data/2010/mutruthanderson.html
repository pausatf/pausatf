Ruth Anderson Returns to California!

Ultrarunning pioneer and longtime PA/USATF member, Ruth Anderson, 80, has
notified us that she and her husband, John, have moved to Eureka, Calif.
from Eugene, Ore.

The former longtime resident of Oakland, Calif. (before moving to Eugene) will
forever be honored and recognized by the Pacific Association, whose Ultra Grand
Prix honors her with its annual (now 25th) Ruth Anderson Ultra Classic
at Lake Merced in San Francisco, and by the USATF with its annual Ruth
Anderson Award bestowed upon the most outstanding female ultrarunner each year.

Welcome back to California, Ruth!
