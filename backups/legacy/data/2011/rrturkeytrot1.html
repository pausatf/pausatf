Run/Walk for Health, Hope, and a Home
November 24, 2011 (Thanksgiving)
Applied Materials, in partnership with the Silicon Valley Leadership Group, The City of San Jose, the County of Santa Clara, and Central Labor Council, present a 5K and 10K family fun event on Thanksgiving Day. The event benefits three local charities (Santa Clara County Children's Health Initiative, Second Harvest Food Bank, and the Housing Trust of Santa Clara County).
