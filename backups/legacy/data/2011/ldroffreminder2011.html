All Pacific Association LDR Clubs:
Two IMPORTANT reminders

REMINDER #1

All PA/USATF clubs who have not renewed their membership dues for 2011 should do so NOW. To renew go to:

www.pausatf.org/data/clubrenewal.html

 

REMINDER #2

As of March 1, 2011 PA/USATF clubs are required to have a minimum number of certified USATF officials (with an LDR/XC specialty) on their club roster to score team points on the PA Road and Cross Country Grand Prix circuits. This requirement applies if your club had 25 or more club members compete on the Road and Cross Country Grand Prix circuits (combined) in 2010. Applicable clubs must meet this officials requirement by March 1 or they will not be scored until they do so. See the list below to see if this requirement applies to your club.

Individuals may obtain USATF LDR Officials certification by attending one of two upcoming Beginning Long Distance Running Officials Clinics:

  • Saturday, January 29, 2011 at U.C. Davis (9:00 a.m.-noon)
  • Sunday, January 30, 2011 at Serra High School/San Mateo (10:00 a.m.-1:00 p.m.)
  • The clinic fee is $13 per individual and USATF rulebooks are included.
  • Registration is available on Active.com at the following link:
    www.active.com/running-membership/folsom-ca/pacific-association-officials-clinics-2011

More information about this requirement is available at:

www.pausatf.org/data/officials/offldrclinics.pdf

Visit the PA/USATF Officials page for more information about officiating:

www.pausatf.org/indexofficials.html
