2011 Pacific Association Junior Olympic XC Meet Schedule Change

The schedule for the 2011 Pacific Association Junior Olympic Cross Country Championships on November 20 has been changed. The two non-USATF races for elementary and middle schoolers have been dropped due to lack of sufficient entries. The new meet schedule is:

7:30: Packet Pickup
8:45: Presentation of PAUSATF Grand Prix Competition Winners
9:00 : Course Walks
10:00: PAUSATF Jr. Olympic Sub-Bantam Girls and Boys 2K race
10:25: PAUSATF Jr. Olympic Bantam Girls 3K race
10:50: PAUSATF Jr. Olympic Bantam Boys 3K race
11:15: PAUSATF Jr. Olympic Midget Girls 3K race
11:40: PAUSATF Jr. Olympic Midget Boys 3K race
12:05: PAUSATF Jr. Olympic Youth Girls 4K race
12:30: PAUSATF Jr. Olympic Youth Boys 4K race
12:55: PAUSATF Jr. Olympic Intermediate Girls & Boys and Young Women & Men 5K

(Note: The four Intermediate and Young races will be combined unless there are more than 25 entrants per age/gender division or the Meet Director determines otherwise. Qualifying and scoring will be by each age group.)

The entry deadline for the meet remains the same:

ENTRY DEADLINE IS 11:59:59 PM, WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 16, 2010
***THERE IS NO MAIL-IN REGISTRATION***
***THERE IS NO MEET DAY REGISTRATION***
***THERE IS NO LATE REGISTRATION***

For additional information about the meet, please see the meet information sheet posted at the 2011 Youth Cross Country Meet Schedule.
