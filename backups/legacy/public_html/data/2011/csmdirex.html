September 20 Board or Athletics meeting site directions

College of San Mateo COLLEGE CENTER (Building 10) 1600 W. Hillsdale Blvd, San Mateo College Heights Conference Room (468) on Fourth Floor This is the new towering building overlooking the athletic fields --and the Bay plus San Francisco.

DIRECTIONS: Exit from Highway 92 (San Mateo Bridge Freeway) at the top of the hill in San Mateo --at Hillsdale Blvd. Keep to your right (north) up the hill (through one signal light) to the college entrance. Keep RIGHT on the East Perimeter Road --towards the track / stadium area. Do NOT turn right to go down to the track, however. You will then see the College Center straight ahead on the left side of East Perimeter Road.

PARKING

Park in any of the Bulldog lots on the right side of the Perimeter Road: Bulldog Lot 9A is across the road from College Center and the closest. Bulldog Lot 9B is the softball lot, one level below (Lot 9A). Bulldog Lot 9D is the baseball/track lot just above the stadium.

There may be some parking spaces still available in the Forum Lot at the entrance to College Center. (That is metered hourly parking.) I will have parking permits available so you do not need to pay for parking in any of the lots. You should obtain a permit from me regardless of where you park. Even the athletic lots can be (randomly) enforced at night.

Note: The tennis courts/Stadium Lot 11 where you may have parked for track meets is OK too. But it is a bit longer uphill walk to College Center from that lot.
