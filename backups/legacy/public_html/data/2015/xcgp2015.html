Directions

 

Take Hwy 17 fron San Jose to Santa Cruz. Take Hwy 1 North towards Half Moon Bay, turn right on Bay St.

Proceed up to UCSC Main Entrance at the corner of High and Bay St., go straight into campus. Turn left on Hagar Dr,  go past the Fieldhouse, turn left on McLaughlin Dr. and then turn right on Chinquapin Rd. Turn right just before the Firehouse into the Crown College parking lot. Registration is in the small parking area above the Firehouse.

LIMITED PARKING NOTICE: The Crown College parking lot (the closest lot) has construction/repaving equipment sited there, so parking in that lot is extremely limited. Arrive early and expect to have to park in the East Remote parking lot (#104 as shown on the campus map) on Hagar Drive. It’s about a mile walk away from the race registration which is lot #156 on Chinquapin Road above the Firehouse.

Expect to have a shuttle run for those that arrive late. Campus Map

Right after entrance to Oak Knolls area parking.

Beer mugs to first 325 finishers. Make sure car is parked off the roadway. 

This race is a memorial to John Lawson, a Tamalpa Runner who, at 44, died suddenly of a heart attack in 2000.

We strongly suggest you set up your team meeting area in the South Bleachers of the Polo Fields.  You can erect pop-up canopies, fly your team flag.  Be good collegiate types and clean up afterwards.  Portapotties are South of the bleachers through the South Tunnel at the West end of the bleachers, please use the portapots before using the existing building toilets, which are subject to flooding if used too heavily (that happened last time, not good). At this location you are about 200 yards East from the finish and 600 yards West from the start.
