CIM 2014 Gun vs. Net Time Scoring Information



Per conversations with Mark Winitz, George Kleeman & CIM Race Director:
  • The CIM is using net times for the CIM age division awards this year, although gun times will be used for prize money winners: Open Division top 5 finishers and Masters Division top 3 finishers.
  • All PA/USATF CIM Finishers will be scored by gun times per USATF rules.
  • All the above applies to both men and women finishers.

Please submit questions about these rules to LDR Scorer Tom Bernhard.
