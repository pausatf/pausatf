2014 PA/USATF Race Walk Schedule

Includes PA Race Walk Grand Prix Championship Events, Results and Standings

Updated 10/31/2014

NOTES:
  • Please email Jon Price to sign up as a JUDGE, OFFICIAL, or VOLUNTEER. Thanks.
  • PA RW Grand Prix Championships are noted in red type.
  • Results, summaries and updated standings will be added to these event listings as they become available.
  • Races in italics are outside of the Pacific Association's area. Check www.usatf.org or the race websites for details on those races. We expect other USATF associations in the Western Region to host a 5,000-meter championship (Albuquerque), possibly a 30-km championship (San Diego), and a one-mile championship (date and location to be determined).
  • There will be practice race walks scheduled at about 12:15 or 12:30 p.m. every Saturday in January and February at Los Gatos High School (http://www.losgatostrackclub.com/schedule--news.html)> The first PA-USATF Grand Prix Championship race walk of 2014 will be there on February 15.

      August 10 (Sunday morning) - Western Region & PA Grand Prix Championship 3,000-meter RACE WALK
      Registration Form
      Location: South Lake Tahoe in conjunction with the August 8-10 (Friday-Sunday) Lake Tahoe Track & Field Festival (Youth - Open - Masters). This is the same weekend as Hot August Nights in Reno and Sparks, but there will be plenty of hotel rooms available in South Lake Tahoe and Stateline, Nevada. The elevation (6,272 feet = 1,912 meters) is excellent for training; the weather will be cool (temperature at race time between 55 and 70 degrees F); and the race will be short and fast. The race will be on the Community Youth Sports Complex track at South Tahoe Middle School (site of the 1968 Olympic Trials), 2940 Lake Tahoe Boulevard (U.S. Highway 50), South Lake Tahoe, CA 96150 - entrance to the track off A1 Tahoe Boulevard. The meet director is Anthony Davis anttrack@charter.net, and the race walks are hosted by the Pacific Racewalking Organization (contact: Beth and Jon Price, aejprice@att.net or jprice@unr.edu. The women's race starts at 9:30 AM, and the men's race will start immediately afterward, at about 10:20 AM.

      Race Walk Events (January to July, 2014)



      January 11 (Saturday) - Clinic for new race walk officials and/or judges: Introduction to Race Walk Officiating (covering the jobs of recorder, DQ board operator, and chief lap scorer) and Introduction to Race Walk Judging, American River College, Sacramento

      January 18 (Saturday) - 1,500-meter Race Walk (separate male and female races if sufficient numbers of race walkers compete) as part the Cal All Comers meet, University of California, Berkeley. See details; ($5 registration). Track events are on a rolling schedule, starting at 11:00 a.m.. The race walks will occur later on the schedule - after several running and hurdle events, after the 200-meter sprints, and before the 3,200-meter runs and 4x400-meter relays. You must register before 2:00 p.m., and the race walks could begin about then.

      February 1 (Saturday) - Clinic for new race walk officials and/or judges: Introduction to Race Walk Officiating (covering the jobs of recorder, DQ board operator, and chief lap scorer) and Introduction to Race Walk Judging, Fresno area - for officials from the Central California Association of USATF.

      February 9 (Sunday) - Clinic for new race walk officials and/or judges: Introduction to Race Walk Officiating (covering the jobs of recorder, DQ board operator, and chief lap scorer) and Introduction to Race Walk Judging, Serra High School, San Mateo
