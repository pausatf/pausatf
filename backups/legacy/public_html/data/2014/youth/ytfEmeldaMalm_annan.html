National Champion Emelda Malm-Annan!

2014 National Junior Olympics Track & Field Championships



PA's Golden State Throwers Youth Team's National Champion: Emelda Malm-Annan! She won the USATF National Championship Hammer Throw
in the 17-18 youth women's division with a throw of 158'2".

Emelda is going into her senior year at Monterey Trail High School in Elk Grove, CA and is planning to return to the National Junior Olympics next year.

Emelda Malm-Annan and Coach Mike Curry.
