Isabella Fauria Sets PA Record, Wins 3 Golds!

2014 National Junior Olympics Track & Field Championships





Isabella Fauria, from the Pacific Association, girls age group 11-12, won 3 National Championships
at the 2014 USATF Junior Olympics at Humble Texas. She brought home the Gold with a Pacific Association
Record in the 800 meters (2:19.06), and won the Gold in the 1500 meters (4:52.91) and 3000 meters (10:27.40).
