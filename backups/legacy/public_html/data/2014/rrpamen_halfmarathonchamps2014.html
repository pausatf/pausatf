2014 USATF Half Marathon Championships:

PA Men and PA-Connected Women Run Strong!

  

PA men finished strong at the USATF Half Marathon Championships in Houston yesterday (1/19/2014): Shadrack Biwott goes sub 1:02 for a 7th place finish, Sergio Reyes 23rd (1:04:06), Daniel Tapia 26th (1:04:32), Anthony Solis 28th (1:04:35). Results

PA-connected women also had excellent times: Steph Dinius was 8th in 1:13:28 (OT qualifier), Alia Gray 9th (1:13:34), Kara Lubieniecki 10th (1:13:38), and Sara Hall 11th (1:13:38). Results
