From Stephanie Brown Trafton: Support AB 2323!


Hi there!

It's May! Open Track season in full swing.

In other news, I have been invite to speak in the state assembly in support of AB 2323 which gives Olympic/Paralympic medalists an exemption from the state tax on their medals and the USOC honorarium received. We need lots of support and the best way to show your support is to download this letter, sign it and email to Jonathan Hughes, Assembly Fellow, Office of Assemblyman Jeff Gorell, 44th District Jonathan: Hughes@asm.ca.gov. Use the subject line: AB 2323 (Gorell) - Support

Thank you!

Stephanie
