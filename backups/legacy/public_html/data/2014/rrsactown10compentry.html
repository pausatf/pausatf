PA/USATF 2014 SACTOWN 10 COMPED ENTRY INSTRUCTIONS

For PA/USATF Runners who are on the 2014 Road Race Grand Prix comped entry list. (Updated 3/21/14)
  • Sactown 10 Race Management sent out an email with the comp code and instructions on January 7.
  • If you did not receive this email and need the code, email Sactown 10 Technical Director Kevin Pool, and he will email it to you.
  • ONLY runners on the 2014 comped entry list may use this procedure. These entries are checked to confirm comped entry status.
  • There is not a field in the form for your PA team. This is not necessary since your team captain does the team submissions, which are cross-checked with the PA team database.
  • Event website for race information.

Good luck with your race!
