2013 California International Marathon

PA/USATF Runner Comped Entries Instructions


  • The 2013 CIM is the PA/USATF Road Racing Grand Prix (long division) Marathon Championship for all age divisions.
  • Click here to view PA/USATF runners eligible for 2013 Road Racing Grand Prix long division event comped
    entries. Short division event comped entry runners on this list are not eligible for a CIM comped entry.
  • If you are on the long division comped entry list, email the CIM's Elite Athlete Coordinator, Danielle Domenichelli; include your
    name and your request for the comped entry code that you will use when you register. She will then
    email you the code.
  • To register, go to this site.
  • To learn more about CIM elite runner status, click here for information.
