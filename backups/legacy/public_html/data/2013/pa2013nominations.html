Call for Nominations

for USATF-Pacific Board of Athletics Officers


Dear USATF-Pacific member,

Nominations are for open the following Association officers:
  • President
  • Vice President
  • Secretary
  • Treasurer
Each officer serves a two year term beginning 1 June. For a full description of each officer's duties refer to: PAUSATF Association Bylaws, and scroll down to Article 6.

Please e-mail all nominations (you may self-nominate) to the Association office at pausatf@aol.com. To complete the nomination, also have someone else submit an e-mail seconding the nomination.

Nominations close Tuesday, 9 April at 11:59 p.m.

If you have any questions please contact the Association office at pausatf@aol.com.

Please consider nominating yourself or someone else whom you think would contribute in a leadership role to our Association and Sports!
