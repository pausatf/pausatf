Extension of nominations for Youth Committee positions

Dear PAUSATF Members,

Because there has been a miscommunication concerning the Youth Committee nomination process, the USATF Pacific Board of Directors has decided to extend the nomination period for all Youth Committee positions for an additional 10 days through 21 July at 11:59pm.

Note that any Association member, 18 years or older can nominate or self-nominate a candidate for office. If you wish to nominate yourself or someone else please send your nomination to pausatf@aol.com by 21 July at 11:59pm. You should receive a confirmation that your nomination has been received. If that doesn't occur within one day please resubmit the nomination or call the association office at 916.983-4715.
