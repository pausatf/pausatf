Hello everyone,

I hope all is well with you so far this season. My name is Darius Strickland, Head Coach of the Phoenix Gliders Track and Field and Cross Country Club. I am your new Youth Cross Country Chairperson for the Pacific Association. I am hoping to get to know and work with each and everyone of you before the end of this season.

Please feel free to call me 707-624-0492 or email me at phoenixgliders@ymail.com if you have any suggestions or questions. I want this season to be a joyful one. And thank you so much for electing me to the position of Youth Cross Country Chairperson.
