PA LDR Committee 2010 Road Racing Travel Grants

Updated 6/9/2010
LDR TRAVEL GRANT (MARATHON) APP DEADLINE EXTENDED

Please note that the deadline to apply for this travel grant has been
extended to July 22, 2010.

PA/USATF will grant $250 each to a woman and a man to travel
to the marathon of their choice between September 1, 2010 and January
15, 2011. The objective of this travel opportunity is to qualify for the
2010 U.S. Olympic Marathon Trials (if the athlete has not already done
so).  Selection criteria is as follows:

1. Athletes must apply by 7/22/10

2. The athlete shall have no other source of funding,

3. Grant shall be given to the athlete who has run the fastest marathon
between 10/1/09 and 4/30/10 under the AADP standards of 2:24 (men)
and 2:51 (women).

4. If no one applies who has run such a marathon, grant shall be given
to the athlete with the highest Purdy-Gardner score in any race 10K
or longer between 10/1/09 and 4/30/10.

Applications should be made to the LDR chairs: women, Katie Wasilenko
katiewaz@gmail.com; men, Tyler Abbott tabbott@gguol.ggu.edu; masters Lloyd
Stephenson fstmstr@mac.com.
