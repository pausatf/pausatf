Every March, at the beginning of every new season roughly 600 Pacific Association athletes in all Divisions do (pretty much) the exact same thing. Lace up their spikes for the start of the USATF Track Season and for a wild hope in their heart that they will exceed all expectations and achieve something that they have never done before.

The 2009 PAUSATF Season has everything that makes for an epic season. It has your insanely favored, your once-great-but-now-I-got-something-to-prove as well as the always looming wildcard contingent.

As for our part in this epic season. we'll be bringing you the 1st Annual South Bay Youth Sectional. Hosted in the East Hills of San Jose, California. The second stop on the PAUSA Track & Field Calendar. The South Bay Sectional will be Held March 21, 2009 at Mt Pleasant High School. Home of Two-time State and 2008 Junior Worlds 100 Meter
