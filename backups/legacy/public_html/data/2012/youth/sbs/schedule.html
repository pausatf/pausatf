Sanctioned By the USATF and the Pacific Association, in compliance with USATF rules a standards age-group schedule will be conducted.

Our objective is to create an environment where the opportunity is much greater for developing athletes to succeed early in the season. Fair and Fun competition is essential in all sports and critical in the development of interest that grows with success.

Our efforts go into creating early season success for many of our inexperience athletes. While continuing to provide the framework of an abiding commitment, as our more experienced athletes continue to succeed and qualify for the Junior Olympic and Youth National Championships.

We ask that you help to support our Youth and thier efforts as we encourage a healthy growth and respect for our sport, athletes and the community that supports them.
