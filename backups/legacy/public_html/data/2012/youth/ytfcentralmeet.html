Buffalo Babes & Oak Hill Score Top Marks

at Central Valley Road Runners Meet



From Joe Hartman
NEWSFLASH FROM MANTECA

Central Valley Roadrunners Meet - Sierra HS - Manteca, CA
  • Fiona O'Keeffe wins great duel with Julia Bounds in the 3000m - 10:11 to 10:13... the two fastest Youth Girls times in the nation*
  • Annaka Green edges Fiona in the 1500m - 4:44 to 4:45... the two fastest Youth Girls times in the nation*
  • Maggie Bell takes over national #1 time* in the 800m - 2:16.56 - Annaka 2nd in 2:18.01

National Youth Girls ('98/'99) Rankings *from www.eliteyouth.com
  • 800m - 1st Maggie 2:16.56 2nd Danae Rivers (CT) 3rd Annaka 2:18.01
  • 1500m - 1st Annaka 4:44.27 2nd Fiona 4:45.79 3rd Maggie 4:47.21
  • 3000m - 1st Fiona 10:11.80 2nd Julia 10:13.20 3rd Brooke Starn

8 of these 9 times are from either the Buffalo Babes or Oak Hill Racing. Add Lauren Jacob, Talley Hill, Megan McCandless, Cat Kiser, Chase Worthen, & the Derry and Warwick twins to that mix and wow... that's some amazing 8th graders in the PA.
