PA's Suzanna Bon takes 5th in the IAU World Championship 24-Hour Race


As 2nd Team USA Woman Finisher, Suzanna helps bring home the Gold!



The International Association of Ultrarunners organized the 9th 24 Hour World and 18th European Championships on Sepetmber 8-9th 2012 in Katowice, Poland.

A record number of athletes and federations participated in the event. The championships saw 254 athletes from 34 nations vying for the top spot in the race.

The race was organized on a 1544.87 m course around Park Slozi in Chozin. Individual countries had set up their refreshments to support their athletes. The cooler temperatures during the day and night combined with a relatively flat course allowed the athletes to set several national records and personal bests.

Bon, age 48, is from Sonoma, CA, is a mother of five, and a former elementary school teacher. She has been an ultra running standut since 2003 and her first 24 hour race was an amazing 134.7-miles to win the San Francisco 24 Hour Race in 2009. She competed with Team USA in the 2010 World 24-Hour event held in France, where she finished 3rd American woman and 17th overall. Her distance at the 2012 championships (143.583 miles) is a personal best by five miles.
