PA/USATF Men's LDR Chair Position Filled



At the 9/23/2012 meeting of the PA/USATF LDR committee, Tom Bernhard was elected the new Men's LDR Chair.
Tom succeeds Tyler Abbott who held the position for 12 years. Tyler will continue as the committee's secretary and parliamentarian.
Tom wishes to thank Tyler for his exceptional performance as the Men's Chair and hopes he can do half as well executing the duties of the position.
