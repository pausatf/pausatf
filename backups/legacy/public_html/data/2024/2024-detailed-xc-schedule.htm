Registration is on the trail at the top of the hill above the small parking area above the Firehouse.		 Start and finish in Lindley Meadow. Two-mile loops in Polo Field area of GGP. All comped athletes must pre-register. Cash or checks only on race day. PA course records: Open men: Chris Frias 15:21 (2014). Open women: Teresa McWalters 17:36 (2017). 40+ men: Neville Davey 16:00 (2015). 40+ women: Valerie Young 18:57 (2011). 50+ men: Brian Pilcher 17:28 (2008). 50+ women: Cathy Dubay 20:23 (2017). 60+ men: Jacob Nur 18:33 (2017). 60+ women: Joannie Siegler 21:46 (2017). 70+ men: Hans Schmid 22:15 (2013). 70+ women: Melody-Anne Schulz 25:42 (2013).

Parking available, carpooling encouraged.

China Camp course, a 4.25 mile eyeloop course, rolling with lots of single track. About 1/4 mile of road.

Make sure car is parked off the roadway. 

This race is a memorial to John Lawson, a Tamalpa Runner who, at 44, died suddenly of a heart attack in 2000.		   The 2024 Course will return to the course used in 2022 and before. A slight difference is made on the west end of the course due to erosion on the trail on the water. Course has been shortened from 4.2 miles to 4.1 miles. Start will take place on large grass field. An updated course map can be found here. Loop course, rolling hills. $75 bonus for breaking any age group course record. 1) There will be a new cash award for master women. This is called the The Nancy Thomas Women’s Masters Performance Award. This $100 cash award will be given each year to the masters woman runner with the highest age-graded performance. The award is funded from a bequest from Nancy Thomas, a long-time member of the Impala Racing Team who loved to train hard and race even harder, especially in cross country events. ALL masters women competitors are eligible for the award, regardless of team affiliation.

 2) This championship welcomes collegiate runners. Coaches or captains email Irene Herman, with a list of runners, date of birth so that she will register & accomodate them in the results. Individual collegiate runners can do the same. Please follow the deadline dates of November 14, by 5:00pm.

3) We are using the original renovated west end course. 2x2 mile loop for women and 3x2 mile loop for open men (see map); 5 mile course for Masters Men (see map).
