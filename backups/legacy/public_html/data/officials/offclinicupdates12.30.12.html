Fundamentals of FIELD Officiating...$15.00...

  • For Apprentice and Association Level officials who have already taken the Introduction to Track & Field Officiating Clinic, who have at least two years of experience and who want to learn more about officiating the horizontal jumps (long jump and triple jump), the vertical jumps (high jump and pole vault) and the throws (shot put, discus throw, javelin throw and hammer throw.) Everyone bring your own lunch. There will be NO SEPARATE LUNCH break during this six hour class…so bring your own lunch.

Fundamentals of TRACK Officiating...$15.00...

  • For Apprentice and Association Level officials who have already taken the Introduction to Track & Field Officiating Clinic, who have at least two years of experienceand who want to learn more about officiating track events - umpiring, starting, clerking, lap scoring, timing, finish line and marshaling duties. There will be NO SEPARATE LUNCH break during this six hour class... so bring your own lunch.

Implement Inspection...$15.00...

  • For all Association, National and Master Level THROWS officials who have not yet taken the clinic. While you may never actually weigh and measure throwing implements, everyone who wants to advance from Association to National or from National to Master level in the throws needs to attend this class. You need to understand what goes on with the implements before they arrive on the field, and how you should handle the implements once they arrive.
  • Saturday, February 2, 2013 from 8:00 a.m. to completion at U.C. Davis, [This class is held in conjunction with an All Comer Meet at U.C. Davis.]
