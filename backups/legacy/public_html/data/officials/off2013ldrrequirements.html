CLUB OFFICIALS REQUIREMENTS - 2013

Revised January 7, 2013

All Pacific Association LDR Clubs:

PA/USATF clubs are required to have a minimum number of certified USATF officials (with an LDR/XC specialty) on their club roster to score team points on the PA Road and Cross Country Grand Prix circuits. This requirement applies if your club had 25 or more "unique" club members compete on the Road and Cross Country Grand Prix circuits (combined) in 2012. Applicable clubs must be in compliance with this requirement for officials by March 1, 2013 or they will not be scored until they do so. Refer to the list below to see if this requirement applies to your club and your current officials status.

IMPORTANT: All current PA/USATF LDR officials must attend a recertification clinic to retain their officials certification for the 2013-2016 Olympiad. Only Apprentice level LDR officials who first received an LDR officials certification in 2012 are exempt from this recertification process. All LDR officials have received e-mail notification and details about 2013 recertification clinics. LDR officials may sign up for these recertification clinics by clicking here.

Individuals who want to become a USATF LDR Official may attend the following Introduction to Long Distance Running Officials Clinic:
Sunday, February 10, 2013 at Serra High School/San Mateo (10:30 a.m. - 1:30 p.m.)

Registration for this introductory clinic is available by clicking here.

For more information about club officials requirements, contact Mark Winitz (PA/USATF LDR Officials Coordinator).

Look below to see your club's 2013 officials requirements!
