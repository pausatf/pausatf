Want your running history to be included in a scientific study?  Have 15 minutes to spare?  There is still time to participate in the Stanford Injury Prevention Survey!


Please send any survey-related questions and comments to the medical student researchers involved in the study: 
Kierann Smith, kierann@stanford.edu
