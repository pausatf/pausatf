Information for Entering for the USATF 2010

National Club Track & Field Championships

The club contact listed on your club’s USATF Club Membership Application is the person who has the ability to process the entries.

If someone else on the club wants to enter athletes then the main contact person needs to log in to the Club Management Area:

https://www.usatf.org/mgmt/clubs/index.asp

and add that person as someone who can process entries.
