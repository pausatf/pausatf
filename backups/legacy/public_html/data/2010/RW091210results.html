Team Results: Team grand prix score is the average of the top 3 age grade percentages.
No team had the necessary 3 participants in the grand prix race (20K) in order to score team points in this race.
Race conditions: Partly Cloudy, Calm, 60 degrees warming up to about 70, on a 2.5K out and back loop course on a smooth bike path
Judges: Ann Gerhardt (chief judge), Beth Price, Jon Price, Liesbeth Mathieu, Becky Klein, Helen Storrs, Art Klein
Recorder: Ajay Padgaonkar
And numerous other volunteers - runner, lap counters, water table, … wihtout whom this race would not have been possible.
A few other brief notes: It was an honor to have Olympian John Nunn and his coach Enrique Pena participate in this race.
We look forward to hosting him again in the near future, likely in a special 20K race as part of the 10K on October 10th, in his continued bid to make the national team. Other celebrity shout-outs: former national team members Mark Green and Susan Armenta also both put in good performances. And the youth representing the LSI Sprint team had excellent races in their first 10K competition.
