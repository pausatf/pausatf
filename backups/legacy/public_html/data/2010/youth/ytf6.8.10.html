Important Packet Pick Up Notices for the

PA Championships Track & Field Meet

1) PA Athlete  Changes Can be made at Early Packet Pick-up  

If you would  like to change an athlete's competition event after you completed their  online registration for the Pacific Association Youth Championships on June  12-13 at Santa Rosa  High School , you may  come to early packet pick-up to make this change.  This is the only time  that changes can be made.  There will be no refunds if you desire to  cancel an event.  There will be no last minute registration of athletes.  This is for event changes only.  This opportunity will not be offered on  Saturday or Sunday. 

  • Date:  Friday, June 11
  • Time:  5:00 - 7:00 pm
  • Location:  Comfort Inn, 2632 Cleveland Ave. , Santa  Rosa   95403

2) Bring birth validation document if it was submitted within past 2 weeks!

If you submitted a document to the PA office to validate a date of birth in the two weeks prior to this meet, please bring the document with you when you pick up your packet.  This way, if the electronic system is not automatically updated, we can take care of it for you on Friday.
