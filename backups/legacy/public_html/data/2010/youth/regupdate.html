PA Championships Registration

 

Important Registration Information

 

Due to over activity the USATF national membership database has been experiencing problems. As a result we have not been able to electronically register Teams and individuals for the PA Championship meet. If you had your enteries delayed because of the online registration problems. Pleae use Club Manager and email your club or individual entries to Anthony Camargo by 10 p.m. tonight.

 

Important Date of Birth Verification Information

 

Also we have not been able to validate DOB documents. Therefore we will be validating DOB Friday, June 11 between 5:00 – 7:00 pm at the Packet Pickup.

 

Also If you have submitted a document to the PA office to validate a date of birth in the two weeks prior to this meet, please bring the document with you when you pick up your packet Friday, June 11. This way, if the electronic system is not automatically updated, we can take care of it for you on this day.

 

Important Event Change Information

 

If you would like to change an athlete’s competition event after you completed their online registration for the Pacific Association Youth Championships on June 12-13 at Santa Rosa High School , you may come to early packet pick-up to make this change. This is the only time that changes can be made. There will be no refunds if you desire to cancel an event. There will be no last minute registration of athletes. This is for event changes only. This opportunity will not be offered on Saturday or Sunday.

 

  • Date:  Friday, June 11
  • Time:  5:00 - 7:00 pm
  • Location:  Comfort Inn, 2632 Cleveland Ave. , Santa  Rosa   95403
