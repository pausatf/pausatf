Pacific Association Masters Track & Field Committee Report


from Committee Chair, Joy Upshaw Margerum

September 22, 2009


~ Pacific Association Masters Track & Field Championships Meet in Folsom was in the morning session prior to the Golden West Meet. Great Reviews! Officials were happy & so were the athletes...terrific format, could be added this way again next year.

~ Senior Games at Stanford. Well attended & great feedback on time management!  PA Officials were incredible & athletes were impressed with our PA organization.
Nat's Masters News included a booth with Banners promoting our upcoming Nationals for 2010 and our World Meet for 2011 in Sacramento.

~World Meet in Finland. Sacramento Sports Commission was  well represented. Booth was filled with informational material on our 2011 WMA  and other booklets from the California Tourist Industry. Fantastic! Lots of interest & excitement!
