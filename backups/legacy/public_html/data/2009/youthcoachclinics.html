USATF Coaching Education - Level 1 Schools

The USATF Coaching Education Level 1 program is intended as a starting point for coaches.  One of the objectives of the Level 1 program is to put all coaches on common ground (i.e., speaking the same language).  The Level 1 program covers all rudimentary manner by emphasizing fundamentals, rules, safety / risk management, and instruction techniques.  Level 1 certification prepares an individual to coach at junior high schools and high schools, as well as for clubs and junior age divisions.
