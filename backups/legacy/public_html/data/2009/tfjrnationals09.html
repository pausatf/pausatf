PA Junior Athletes Excel at 2009 U.S. Track & Field Championships

June 25-28, 2009

Eugene, Oregon

Report & Quotes courtesy of USATF

2008 World Junior champion Marquise Goodwin won the men's long jump for the second year in a row on his only legal jump with a mark of 7.96m/26-1.5. Just a few hours later, Goodwin came back and competed in the open men's long jump and took fifth with a national high school record jump of 8.18m/26-10. American junior triple jump record holder Will Claye was the runner-up in the junior long jump with a mark of 7.80m/25-7.25. On the women's side, Alitta Boyd won with a leap of 6.28m/20-7.25.

2008 USATF Youth Athlete of the Year Jordan Hasay won her heat of the women's 1,500m in 4:28.96. Blake Shaw has the top time in the men's 1,500m (3:50.75) heading into the final tomorrow. Chanelle Price posted the fastest women's 800m in the rounds with 2:06.32 and Washington State's Joe Abott was the fastest man at 1:49.78.

Jordan Hasay – Winner Junior Women’s 1500M
 
On her race:
“It was really windy on the backstretch, so I was a little worried about that. The plan was to lay low on the first two hundred and then I decided to take the lead and go for it. My coach told me once I got the lead to run 68s or 69s, and that’s what I tried to do.”
 
On her plans after this:“I’m not really sure yet. I might run at the Pan Am’s and I might not. Originally I was just going to run the senior race, so the Pan Ams aren’t really in my schedule. I might just lay low.”

Alitta Boyd - Winner Junior Women’s Triple Jump
 
“This was my best jump since last year. This whole entire year has been going kind of slow, so I’m very happy. That’s what I was planning to do.”
 
On winning the long jump and triple:
“It feels great. I was not expecting the long jump, but I was just feeling good and I jumped good. I’m pleased. They were both PR’s, so it was just a great weekend.”

Alitta Boyd – Winner Junior Women’s Long Jump
  
On her winning jump:
“I did what I had to do out there. I just made sure to get my foot on the board. When I landed it didn’t feel like a good jump, it felt very average but I won and I’m very happy to make the team.”
