USATF National Club Track & Field Championships - Entry Deadline Reminder 

The USATF National Club Track & Field Championships will be held July 10-11 at Icahn Stadium in New York City, N.Y.

 

On-time entries are due on Monday, July 6 by 5:00 p.m. EDT.  Late entries will be accepted until Wednesday, July 8 at 5:00 p.m. EDT.  All on-time entries must be submitted online using the entry link on the meet website. 

 

The current entry list is available on the USATF website.
