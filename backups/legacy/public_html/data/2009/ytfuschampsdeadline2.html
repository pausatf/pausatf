USA Youth Outdoor T&F Championships - Entry Deadline Extended to Friday June 26, 1:59 p.m. PST.

 

June 25, 2009

The entry deadline for next week's USA Youth Outdoor Track & Field Championships is June 26 1:59 p.m. PST.   

The USA Youth Outdoor Track & Field Championships  will begin next Tuesday (June 30) and  run through Sunday, July 5.  Over  2,500 of the nation's top young athletes expected to compete for national titles and personal bests. 
 
There is no late entry period for this event so all entries must be submitted by the new deadline. 
 
Customer service will be available until 7:55 p,m. EDT by calling 317-261-0500.
