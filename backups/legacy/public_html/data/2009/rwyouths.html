2008 National Race Walk Honor Roll

 

The 2008 National Race Walk Honor Roll selectees have been announced.  Six youth from the Pacific Association have been given this honor from the National Race Walk Committee.  These youth were selected for their outstanding performances in national competitions during the 2008 season.
