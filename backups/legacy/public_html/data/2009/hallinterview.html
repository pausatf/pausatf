Ryan and Sarah Hall Interview

Applied Materials Silicon Valley Turkey Trot Race Director Carl Guardino interviewed Ryan and Sara Hall on his "CEO Show" on KLIV Radio (an affiliate of CNN Radio headquartered in San Jose) the week of the race.

Ryan and Sarah Hall Interview

Click on the Nov. 27 show

The show was recorded at Mt. View High School before an audience of high school cross country and track runners. I believe the show is of interest to a wide audience of PA web site visitors.

Sara Hall (formerly Sara Bei) won the 2000 Footlocker National Cross Country Championship when she was a Senior at Montgomery High School in Santa Rosa. She continued on to star at Stanford University where she met Ryan.

They are aso guest speakers at teh 2009 National Junior Olympic Cross Country Championships this weekend in Reno Nevada.

Although both now live in Mammoth Lakes, they definitely have strong ties to the Pacific Association.
