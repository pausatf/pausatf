West Zone Junior Olympic Indoor Championships

Here is your chance to become a USATF youth indoor Junior Olympic Champion! This event is open to all youth athletes with current 2009 USATF memberships.

  • Date: February 15, 2009
  • Time: 7:45 am (first call) / 8:00 am start time
  • Location: Northern Arizona University Skydome (Flagstaff, AZ)
    • Six lane (8 lane straight-away) 300m flat Mondo track
  • Awards: Custom medals (1-3); Ribbons 4-6)
  • Cost: $3.50 per event (before Feb. 11) / $5.00 per event (after midnight Feb. 11)
  • Events: Mile, 60m, 200m, 400m, 800m, 4x300m, 60m Hurdles, LJ, HJ, SP, PV
    • NOTE: Sub-Bantam only 60m, 200m, 4x300m, LJ
  • Register: Online at CoachO entries at http://www.coacho.com/main/index.html
  • Meet details @ http://www.usatf.org/assoc/az/tf/info-IC-09.html
