 Dublin HS T&F All-Comers

Dates and info are announced!

2009 Dublin High School All-Comers Track & Field Meets

Where:     Dublin High School
     8151 Village Parkway
     Dublin , CA

Dates:     Thursdays    
    June 11, 18 & 25
    July 2, 9, 16, 23 & 30
    August 6 & 13

Divisions:    Youth, High School, Open & Masters

Track & Timing:    8 lane all-weather track & Fully Automatic Timing (FAT)

Fee:    $4 for athletes (unlimited events) - $3 for each athlete of groups of 10 or more from the same club or team (arriving together – must arrange prior to the day of the meet)
    $2 for spectators

Results:    Available at http://www.dublincrosscountry.com/  and http://www.dyestatcal.com/.

Parking:    Free parking available on campus as well as the street.

Notes:    3/16 needle spikes required and will be checked at gate.  Spikes will be sold at the Snack Bar.  USATF rules are in effect.  Snacks and refreshments will be available. Meet will be conducted rain or shine.  Gates open at 4:00 PM.

Running Events Order (starts at 5:00 PM)
400 meter relay
1600 meter run
100/110 meter high hurdles
400 meter run
100 meter run
800 meter run
300 meter low/intermediate hurdles
200 meter run
3200 meter run
1600 meter relay

This is a rolling schedule.  Girls then boys then women then men compete in the running events.

Field Events
5:00 PM – Discus, High Jump & Long Jump
6:00 PM – Shot Put, Pole Vault & Triple Jump

For more information contact meet director Chris Williams at coachchriswilliams@yahoo.com.
