2009 Coaches Clinics Scholarship Winners Announced

 

The Pacific Association’s Coach’s Committee offered 20 scholarships to any PA/USATF coach (any sport discipline) who qualified to attend the January 2009 USATF Level 1 Coach’s Clinic.  The scholarship value equals $125 per winner.  We are pleased to announce that 14 recipients (74%) are directly involved with youth club coaching.  Congratulations to these scholarship winners and special thanks to Dave Shrock and the PA Coach’s Committee for providing these scholarship opportunities!
