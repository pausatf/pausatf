PA Officials Dick Connors and Leon Glover Honored at Stanford Invite

from Bruce Colman, PA Officials Chair

At their Invitational Track and Field meet on April 5, 2008 Stanford University’s track coaches honored Master Officials Dick Connors and Leon Glover with silver plaques and an on-field ceremony.

Watch for an in-depth story and photos about the ceremony, and learn about the many accomplishments by these two valued Pacific Association officials, in the May/June '08 issue of California Track & Running News.
