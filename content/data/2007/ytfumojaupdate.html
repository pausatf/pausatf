Umoja meet – throws events added

Saturday, May 5 @ Santa Clara HS, Umoja will be adding the discus for Midget and older age groups as well as the 600mg turbo javelin for Youth Boys/Girls and Open Girls.  If you’d like to modify your entries for this meet please contact the Umoja Meet Director at meet.director@umojatrackclub.com.

Athletes will need to supply their own discus; however, the 600mg turbo javelins will be provided at the meet for competition use.

posted 5/2/2007
